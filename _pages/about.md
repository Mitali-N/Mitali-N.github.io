I am a second-year Computer Science Master's student advised by Dr. Daochen Wang at the University of British Columbia. My current research focus is in theoretical quantum computing with applications to quantum chemistry and many-body physics. I have dabbled previously in quantum algorithms, cryptography, and quantum machine learning, and remain curious about using AI for quantum research.

I am also passionate about community and service, particularly increasing interest in and accessibility of STEM research for people traditionally underrepresented in our field.
